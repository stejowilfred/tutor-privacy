Tutor – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Tutor ("the app") is an attendance and fee tracking app for tuition teachers and tutors. It is developed by Stejo ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Data we collect

  • Tutor account: your name, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash) and an optional profile photo.
  • Records you enter: classes, students' names, grades, optional student photos, attendance records, and fee and payment records (amounts and dates marked manually by you).

The app has no payment gateway. We do not collect card, bank or UPI details. We do not collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app has no ads or analytics trackers.

How we use it

Data is used only to provide the app's features: signing you in, showing and saving your classes, attendance and fee records, and sending a one-time code to your email if you reset your password. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on our backend server (hosted on Render), in a MongoDB database, and photos on Cloudinary. Password-reset emails are sent through an email (SMTP) provider. These providers process data only to deliver the service. Data is sent over HTTPS.

Camera and photos

The app asks to use your camera or gallery only when you choose to add a profile or student photo.

Students' data

The tutor who adds a student is responsible for having permission to record that student's details (and a parent's or guardian's permission for minors). Student records are visible only to the tutor account that created them.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can edit or remove classes and students inside the app. To delete your account and all associated data, email us at the address below and we will do so within 30 days.

Children

The app is for tutors (adults). It is not directed at children and children do not create accounts.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
